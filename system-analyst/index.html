About Me

Fristaka Putri Prawahyu

I graduated in 2025 with a Diploma in Informatics Management from Universitas Logistik dan Bisnis Internasional (ULBI). I am particularly interested in understanding user needs, analyzing business processes, and translating requirements into clear information system designs.

During my internship at Diskominfo Kabupaten Jombang, within the Aplikasi Informatika division, I worked on the planning and design stage of SIAGA. My work included requirements analysis, business process design, and mockup development. I later implemented and tested SIAGA as my final academic project.

I also hold a System Analyst competency certification from LSP ULBI under BNSP certification standards.
