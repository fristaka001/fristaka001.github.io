About Me

Fristaka Putri Prawahyu

I graduated in 2025 from the D3 Informatics Management program at Universitas Logistik dan Bisnis Internasional (ULBI). I am drawn to the early stages of building an information system: understanding what users need, mapping how a process works, and turning that into a clear design.

During my internship at Diskominfo Kabupaten Jombang in the Aplikasi Informatika division, I worked on the planning stage of SIAGA, including requirements analysis, business process design, and mockups. I later implemented and tested SIAGA as my final academic project.

I also hold a System Analyst certification from LSP ULBI on behalf of BNSP.

  • Education D3 Manajemen Informatika, ULBI (graduated 2025)
  • Certification System Analyst, LSP ULBI on behalf of BNSP
  • Internship Diskominfo Kabupaten Jombang, Aplikasi Informatika division
